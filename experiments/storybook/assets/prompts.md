# 绘本素材生成记录

使用内置 image_gen 工具生成。以下三个提示分别生成独立素材；背景要求为真实透明。原始 PNG 用于视觉评估，尚未完成正式发布所需的体积优化与完整素材覆盖。

## police

```text
Use case: stylized-concept.
Asset type: one reusable transparent PNG game sprite, 1024 by 1024 square canvas, for a child's 10x10 police-and-thief board game displayed at 45–60 pixels per cell.
Style: fresh picture-book illustration, soft gouache fills, crisp simplified silhouettes, restrained paper-grain inside the painted areas only, softly rounded shapes, delicate dark desaturated blue outlines, muted sage green, warm ivory, muted coral and denim blue palette. Gentle light from upper left. Cute and friendly, very readable when tiny. A consistent elevated front view, looking down about 30 degrees, minimal perspective, no isometric diamond tile.
Composition: exactly ONE isolated subject centered, generous uniform transparent padding, complete silhouette and feet/base entirely visible, all details in central 78% of canvas. True transparent background with alpha, no opaque ground or floor, no backdrop, no checkerboard painted into the image, no framing card, no text, no logos, no watermark. No scenery, no props outside requested subject, no drop shadow outside silhouette. No photorealism, no shiny 3D toy material. This is an individual production candidate sprite, not a concept sheet.
Subject: one friendly adult police officer with a big rounded head and a small full body, denim-blue cap with a simple gold shield badge, blue uniform, navy trousers, small black boots, arms comfortably at sides, warm face and calm smile. Front-facing. No weapon, no vehicle, no backpack, no money bag. A broad clean silhouette recognizable as a police officer without lettering.
```
